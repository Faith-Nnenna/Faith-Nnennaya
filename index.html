03 / ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Faith.
I like making things clear.

I’m a communications specialist and content strategist based in Nigeria. I work with businesses and individuals, including founders and people building personal brands. My projects have covered education, technology, branding, travel and health.

I enjoy the thinking as much as the doing: understanding who we’re talking to, what they need and what would make the message useful to them. That carries through everything I do, from planning a campaign to writing its social posts, emails and sales page.

If you need someone who can bring ideas, organise the work and follow through, we’ll probably work well together.
